What We Do

We bridge the gap between theory and practical application for aspiring software developers.

Hands-on Workshops

Regular sessions on Web Development, Mobile Apps (Flutter), Cloud Computing, and AI/ML technologies.

Collaborative coding sprints to solve real-life challenges and compete in national hackathons like SIH.
